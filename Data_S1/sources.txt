MEASUREMENTS

The main catalogue comprises replicated Mudge S2 ORFs of <=100 amino acids.
Mudge S2/S3 denote source sheets, not this article's supplement numbering.
Model-specific filters are applied separately in K562 and HepG2.

Host binding counts distinct RBPs with a same-strand eCLIP peak overlapping
a host-transcript exon, not peaks or binding strength.

The occupancy response is log2(in-frame RPF count+1), not protein yield.
log1p(x)=ln(1+x); binding is ln(1+distinct host RBP count). Abundance is
transcript TPM. Host length is mature spliced RNA length in nucleotides,
not genomic span. GC is the fraction of G+C in the whole host transcript,
not ORF GC. ORF length is in amino acids.

Disorder is mean per-residue metapredict score; pLDDT is a predicted
confidence proxy. The fraction of ORFs passing a mean-disorder threshold
is not a per-ORF residue fraction. Structural SD/SD coefficients standardize
both outcome and predictor; canonical outcome-SD-scaled coefficients do not.

ANALYSIS CONVENTIONS

TPM >=1 was selected after an abundance sweep. Equivalence, host-length,
corrected-reference and local-identity analyses are post hoc, not externally
preregistered. Model formulas, population selection, confidence-interval
conventions and multiplicity families are retained in the corresponding
specification.json files, equivalence specification and analysis code.
Incremental R-squared uses identical rows in full and reduced models.
Refitting pooled-track observations does not recover biological-replicate
uncertainty or establish causal RBP effects.

EQUIVALENCE ANALYSIS

This analysis was specified after the ordinary regression estimates and
confidence intervals had been inspected. It is post hoc, not externally
preregistered.

Outcomes are mean predicted pLDDT and catGRANULE score. Binary foldable and
LLPS-prone outcomes are not included. Complete cases are selected separately
by cell line from the main structural-analysis population.
No TPM >=1 cutoff is applied.

The original model is:
z(outcome) ~ z(ln(1+host RBP count)) + z(ln(ORF amino-acid length))
             + z(host-transcript GC) + z(ln(1+TPM)).

Each quantity is standardized within its cell-specific analysis population,
separately for each outcome and scheme, using the sample SD (ddof=1).
The binding coefficient is outcome SD per SD of transformed binding.
Canonical coefficients divided only by the outcome SD are not comparable
SD-per-SD effects and do not calibrate the equivalence bounds.

The primary bound is +/-0.10 SD/SD; +/-0.05 is a sensitivity bound. These are
practical interpretation thresholds, not biologically validated cutoffs.
The primary covariance estimator clusters by host transcript. Sensitivities
use stable ENSG clusters, transcripts with exactly one analysed ORF, or HC3.

TOST uses alpha=0.05. For an individual unadjusted model, equivalence requires
the 90% confidence interval to fall within the chosen bounds. Difference from
zero and equivalence are distinct: a detectable association can be equivalent
within a chosen margin; nonsignificance alone does not establish equivalence.

The primary family has four outcome-by-cell models. Holm correction is applied
to their TOST p-values (the larger one-sided p-value) at the +/-0.10 bound.
The two one-sided tests form one equivalence test, not two comparisons.
The primary overall equivalence claim requires all four primary models to meet
the criterion; unadjusted and Holm-adjusted decisions are reported separately.
Alternative covariance/population schemes and +/-0.05 are sensitivity analyses,
not additional members of this primary family.

Report coefficients, 90%/95% intervals, one-sided p-values, both bounds'
decisions and cluster counts. A supported bound is not proof of zero association.
For the subsequent host-length adjustments, use the separate model definitions
and adjustment-specific families in posthoc/structural/specification.json.

Generated output: analysis/results/followup14_equivalence.csv.
Host-length generated output: posthoc/structural/results.json.
